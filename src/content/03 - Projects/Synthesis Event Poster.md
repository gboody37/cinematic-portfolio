# Synthesis Tech Event Poster

![Synthesis Poster](/photo/projects/poster-design.jpg)

A modern, minimalist poster design created for "Synthesis Night", an electronic music and technology fusion event. The design utilizes sharp geometric shapes and a striking gold/navy color palette.

## Typography & Colors
- **Main Font**: Inter (Bold/Black)
- **Primary Color**: Deep Navy (#0b1424)
- **Accent Color**: Metallic Gold gradient

## Design Process
The objective was to combine the rigid, structured aesthetics of technology with the fluid, energetic vibe of electronic music. By using overlapping metallic triangles on a dark void background, the poster creates a sense of depth and modern luxury.
