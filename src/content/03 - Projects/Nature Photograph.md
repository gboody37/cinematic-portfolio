# Nature Photograph

![Tranquil Lake](/photo/projects/nature-photo.jpg)

A collection of high-resolution landscape photography captured during golden hour. This specific piece features a tranquil autumn lake reflecting the surrounding mountains and misty trees.

## Project Details
- **Camera**: Sony A7R IV
- **Lens**: 16-35mm f/2.8 GM
- **Editing**: Lightroom Classic (color grading, contrast, dehaze)
- **Location**: Jasper National Park

This shot was timed perfectly to capture the low-lying mist rising from the lake surface just as the sun crested the mountains.
